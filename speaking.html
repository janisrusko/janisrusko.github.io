03

Teaching & professional training

Teaching ranges from recurring university courses to concentrated international programmes and practical sessions for laboratory professionals.

Recurring

University teaching

Guest lectures at the University of Latvia since 2018 on chemical risk assessment, biosensors, non-target screening and DART-MS. Risk-assessment lectures and exposure exercises for Rīga Stradiņš University nutrition students hosted at BIOR. Teaching also at Riga Technical University.

Belgrade · 2024

BeSafeBeeHoney Summer School

An afternoon block of lectures and practicals on EU food law and institutions, risk assessment, chemical hazards, dietary exposure, risk and crisis communication, and persistent organic pollutants in honey.

2024–2025

European Commission BTSF

Tutor on the programme on sampling and analysis of veterinary medicinal products for officials from non-EU countries: residential courses in Puławy, Dublin and Brescia, with modules on residue monitoring plans, Codex and international limits, and import controls.

Professional practice

Technical training

Sessions for inspectors, laboratory colleagues and visiting scientists on non-target screening, analytical capability and food-safety evidence.
