From Data to Decisions

I lead a project building a transparent tool that ranks chemical risks in food. It links monitoring data reported to EFSA with food consumption and toxicological values, and maps every gap in the evidence. Latvia is the first test case.

02

How I work

One process, from scattered evidence to a priority someone can defend.

  1. Synthesise

    Bring monitoring data, consumption data, toxicological values and the literature together, with sources and limits in view.

  2. Assess

    Estimate exposure, weigh risks against benefits, and state the uncertainty.

  3. Build

    Turn the evidence into databases and tools that others can rerun and check.

  4. Communicate

    Explain the reasoning to risk managers, specialists, students and the public.
