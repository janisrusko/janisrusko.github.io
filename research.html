Key questions

Comparability

Can results from different sources be compared?

Only after units, foods and substances are harmonised. Results below the limit are run at zero, half and the full limit, so every rank carries a band.

Gaps

What if a substance has no toxicological value?

It is counted and mapped as a gap, and the gap map is published with the ranking. EFSA's values come first. Anything weaker is labelled.

Exposure

Does presence in food mean risk?

No. Exposure adds up what each population group eats. A food eaten but never measured is a monitoring gap, not a safe food.

Trust

Why trust the ranking?

Every method choice is a switch with a documented default, explained where the number appears. Named results wait for a check against EFSA's published exposure estimates.
