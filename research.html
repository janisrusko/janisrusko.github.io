From Data to Decisions

The hard questions

Comparability

Can results from different sources be compared?

Only once foods, substances, units and quality descriptors are harmonised. That was the first work package.

Gaps

What if a contaminant has no guidance value?

The gap is recorded. Benchmark-dose modelling on existing data is one route. What predictive toxicology can support is still being tested.

Exposure

Does presence in food mean risk?

No. Risk depends on who eats the food, how often and how much, and how concentrations compare with reference points.

Use

Will decision-makers use the output?

Only if it is transparent and easy to read. The tool will be tested with risk assessors, laboratories and authorities.
