Assessment and public decisions

Earlier casework · BIOR
Began 2018

Chemical risk-assessment casework

I prepared chemical, and occasional microbiological, risk assessments and scientific opinions for Latvia's Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Veterinary Service and other authorities. The work covered food-contact materials, process contaminants, infant-suitable mineral water, environmental screening methods, food supplements and other regulatory questions. This is part of my professional history, not my current practice.

Public explanation: how food safety is assessed in Latvia · Delfi

Assessor and toolkit lead
2018–2026

Drinking-water safety

I assessed derogation cases for individual water producers, including a multi-year radionuclide-exposure assessment. I later led the development and delivery of a national drinking-water risk-assessment and monitoring toolkit covering more than 200 parameters against European drinking-water requirements.

Senior researcher and task lead
2019–2026

Risk–benefit assessment of fish consumption

For SEAwise, I compiled and normalised contaminant and nutritional data for commercial fish species. In GreenAgroRes, I led BIOR's task on consumption research, fish sampling and health indicators across four Latvian lakes. The resulting pilot assessment weighed methylmercury and PFAS exposure against nutritional benefits.

First-author study · Foods, 2026
02

Evidence systems and research tools

Work that turns scattered occurrence data and literature into reusable evidence structures, comparison methods and monitoring priorities.

Principal investigator · BIOR
2025–2028

National contaminant-occurrence database and risk prioritisation

I lead the From Data to Decisions postdoctoral project: a harmonised database for Latvian food and feed data linked with food consumption and toxicological reference values. The work includes a combined contaminants taxonomy, a database specification, national monitoring-data access and transparent risk-prioritisation methods.

Project description · BIOR Progress report · January–June 2026

Working Group coordinator and first author
2023–present

Bee-product contaminant evidence

Within COST Action BeSafeBeeHoney, I coordinate literature search, screening and data extraction across five edible bee-product matrices. The synthesis covers 161 studies from 61 countries and more than 27,000 harmonised occurrence records; its manuscript is under review.

BeSafeBeeHoney · COST CA22105 Related glyphosate-occurrence study · Food Control, 2026 · Co-author
03

Analytical methods and laboratory foundations

The technical foundation of the later assessment work: developing, validating and applying methods for complex chemical evidence.

Doctoral researcher · BIOR
2017–2021

Non-target high-resolution mass spectrometry

My PhD brought together non-target screening of food-contact materials, honey phenolics, residue analysis and multi-enzymatic biosensors. The non-target line later continued through collaboration with the US FDA on DART-HRMS screening of food-contact articles.

PhD thesis · University of Latvia, 2021 First-author paper-straw screening study · 2020

Study coordinator, first and corresponding author
2015–2019

Confirmatory method for anticoccidial residues

I coordinated the accredited validation of an LC-Orbitrap-HRMS method for 17 anticoccidial drug residues in poultry meat and eggs. The method was applied in Latvia's national residue-monitoring programme and published after optimisation with experimental design.

Method-development study · Journal of Pharmaceutical and Biomedical Analysis, 2019

Method developer and researcher
2016–2026

Phenolics, honey authenticity and food fraud

I developed UHPLC-Orbitrap-HRMS methods for phenolic compounds, first in dark chocolate and later across roughly 400 Latvian and Georgian honey samples. That work supported published honey-authenticity research and later scientific advice on analytical methods and measurement uncertainty in food-fraud investigations.

Dark-chocolate phenolics · First author, 2019 Latvian honey phenolics · First author, 2021

EU-FORA fellow · CNR Naples
2017–2018

Multi-enzymatic biosensors for pesticide assessment

During a one-year EFSA EU-FORA fellowship, I worked in the laboratory of the CNR Institute of Protein Biochemistry on an automated multi-enzymatic biosensor for organophosphate-pesticide assessment in water and food, alongside training at European food-safety institutions. I pitched the work at EFSA's Risk Assessment Research Assembly in Utrecht in February 2018.

EU-FORA fellowship report · EFSA Journal, 2018

Accredited laboratory analyst · BIOR
2015–2017

Routine analysis as a technical foundation

I worked across gas- and liquid-chromatography services for fatty acids, cholesterol, amino acids, milk-fat purity and honey proline. This period grounded later method development and risk assessment in the practical constraints of accredited testing.
